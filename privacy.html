Privacy policy

DNSE-Gmail · effective 24 October 2026

Who uses this app

DNSE-Gmail is used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google account. It is not offered to the public.

Data accessed

With the read-only Gmail scope (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly) the app searches for messages from noreply@mail.dnse.com.vn that arrive after the owner's trading software requests an OTP, and reads the subject and body of those messages to extract the one-time passcode. No other messages are read.

How the data is used

The passcode is passed, on the owner's computer, to the owner's own DNSE trading software to complete DNSE's email-OTP verification. The passcode and the message contents are not printed, logged, stored or sent anywhere else.

Storage

The only data kept is the OAuth client configuration and the OAuth token, stored in the owner's local operating system credential store (Windows Credential Manager). There is no server and no database.

Sharing

No data is shared with, sold to, or transferred to any third party. The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and the stored token removed with the tool's --delete command.

Tóm tắt (tiếng Việt)

Ứng dụng chỉ dành cho chủ tài khoản. Ứng dụng chỉ có quyền đọc Gmail, chỉ đọc email OTP từ noreply@mail.dnse.com.vn đến sau khi phần mềm giao dịch yêu cầu OTP, và chỉ dùng mã đó trên máy của chủ tài khoản để xác thực với DNSE. Không lưu, không ghi log, không chia sẻ dữ liệu. Token được lưu trong Windows Credential Manager và có thể thu hồi bất cứ lúc nào.
